# Email Thread Comprehension

## Overview
This skill parses multi-turn email dialogues, resolves recipient references, and summarizes conversation context.

## Capabilities
- Traces `In-Reply-To` and `References` headers into structured conversation DAGs.
- Extracts key action items, deadlines, and questions requiring responses.
- Identifies participant roles, historical tone, and communication preferences.
